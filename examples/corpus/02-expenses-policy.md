# Expenses and Travel Policy

Applies to every Halcyon Data employee and contractor. Owned by Dana Okafor, Head
of Finance. Last reviewed January 2025.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount | Who approves |
|---|---|
Under €75 | nobody — submit without a receipt |
€75 to €500 | your line manager |
Above €500 | Dana Okafor, Head of Finance |
Above €5,000 | Dana Okafor **and** the VP of the requesting area |

The €75 receipt-free floor exists because chasing a receipt costs more in
administration than the claim is worth. It is not a per-day allowance: three €70
claims for one meal is still one claim, and splitting an expense to stay under a
threshold is a disciplinary matter.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the Voyager portal. Direct bookings are reimbursed
only when Voyager has no fare on the route, and the exception has to be noted on
the claim.

Per-diem is **€60 per day inside the EU** and **€80 per day outside it**. The
per-diem replaces meal receipts; you cannot claim both. Travel days count at half
rate. Trips longer than ten nights need Dana Okafor's approval in advance,
regardless of cost, because a long trip is a staffing decision rather than an
expense.

## Reimbursement

Submit claims in the month they were incurred. Finance pays approved claims within
**15 business days** of approval, on the next payment run. A claim submitted more
than 90 days after the expense date is rejected; the audit trail for the quarter is
closed by then.

## Equipment

Laptops, monitors and chairs are ordered by IT, not claimed as expenses. Anything
that stores company data is asset-tagged and enrolled in device management before
it is handed over — see [Security](03-security-policy.md), which forbids customer
data on unmanaged devices.

Conference tickets are an expense; conference travel follows the travel rules
above. Training that leads to a certification is approved by your line manager up
to €1,500 per year and by Dana Okafor above that.
